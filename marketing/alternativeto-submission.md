## Full description

> Pomodorian is a free Pomodoro timer with an AI Session Planner: describe what you want to get done in plain language, and it returns an editable list of pomodoro-sized tasks with time estimates (8 languages, powered by Claude).
>
> Everything else is free too: five layerable ambient sounds (rain, cafe, lo-fi, forest, fireplace), focus analytics with a GitHub-style heatmap and CSV/Markdown export, per-day task history with automatic carry-over, drag-and-drop task ordering, keyboard shortcuts, dark and light mode, and Never Dumb, a break mode with 13 short exercises (reasoning drills, movement games, breathing and meditation resets) so breaks sharpen you instead of draining you.
>
> Privacy by design: all data stays in your browser's local storage. No account, no cloud sync, no ads, no tracking of your tasks. Installable as a PWA.
