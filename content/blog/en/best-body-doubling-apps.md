## The 8 body doubling apps, one by one

### 1. Focusmate: one-to-one sessions with a stranger

Focusmate pairs you with one other person for a scheduled video session: you state your goal, work, and report back at the end. The [FAQ lists 25, 50, and 75-minute sessions](https://www.focusmate.com/faq/) and states that a camera is necessary. A [Quiet Mode setting](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/8060080-session-settings-my-task-quiet-mode-and-partner) suits people who prefer not to speak.

According to its [pricing page](https://www.focusmate.com/pricing/), the free plan covers 3 sessions per week with no credit card, and Plus (unlimited) costs $8 per month billed yearly or $12 billed monthly. It runs in the browser, with no native app. Group sessions are a [limited Labs experiment](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/15556809-group-sessions-labs) open to a small group of members.

**Choose it if:** you want one specific person seeing you work, and a free tier you can use every week.

### 2. Flow Club: small hosted groups

Flow Club runs group sessions led by a volunteer host: a short goal check-in, silent work with a shared timer, then a wrap-up. Its [FAQ](https://www.flow.club/faq) describes groups of up to eight participants plus a host, in 60, 90, and 120-minute sessions. Video is encouraged but [turning your camera off is fine](https://help.flow.club/en/articles/782337).

After a 7-day free trial with no card, [pricing](https://help.flow.club/en/articles/782785) is $40 per month or $400 per year, with 50% discounts for students and non-profits. It runs in the browser.

**Choose it if:** you like a little group energy and a host who keeps the session on rails.

### 3. FLOWN: guided group sessions and drop-in rooms

FLOWN's facilitated sessions are called Flocks. The [Flocks page](https://flown.com/flocks) lists a 20-minute Take-Off, a 1-hour Power Hour, a 2-hour Deep Dive with breakout groups, and an all-day silent Drop-In room. Facilitated sessions run on Zoom, others in the browser; [cameras are encouraged but optional](https://help.flown.com/en/articles/8264533-what-can-i-and-can-t-i-do-in-a-focus-session).

The [pricing page](https://flown.com/pricing) shows a 30-day free trial with no card, then $25 per month, $19 per month billed yearly, or $900 lifetime. A limited free plan includes 4 sessions per month plus Fridays.

**Choose it if:** you want varied formats and a long trial to test group body doubling.

### 4. Caveday: structured "Caves"

Caveday runs facilitated sessions called Caves, in 30-minute, 1-hour, and 3-hour formats according to its [homepage](https://www.caveday.org/). After intention-setting, everyone mutes and works, cameras encouraged.

**Choose it if:** you want long blocks for big projects.

The [pricing page](https://www.deepwrk.io/pricing) lists $19 per month, or $12 per month billed yearly, with a 7-day free trial. Deepwrk's blog says you can join group sessions with your camera off.

StudyStream runs open video rooms where people work silently, cameras optional. The [help center](https://help.studystream.live/hc/help/articles/1785847584-plans-and-daily-focus-room-time-limits) describes a Free plan with about 1 hour of focus room time per day, Basic with 4 hours, and unlimited Premium. ## Why body doubling may work (and what we do not know)

Body doubling grew out of ADHD coaching, and most of the support for it is still practitioner experience and personal reports. [CHADD notes](https://chadd.org/adhd-news/adhd-news-adults/adhd-weekly-could-a-body-double-help-you-increase-your-productivity/) that there has not been significant research to validate its benefits or explain how it works. [ADDitude](https://www.additudemag.com/getting-stuff-done-easier-with-a-friend-body-double/) describes the body double as someone who sits with a person with ADHD during a hard task.

The closest older research is on social facilitation. In 1965, psychologist Robert Zajonc proposed in [*Science*](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.149.3681.269) that the presence of others raises arousal, which tends to help on simple or well-practiced tasks and hinder on complex, new ones. A [1983 meta-analysis of 241 studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6356198/) by Bond and Titus found these presence effects to be real but small. Direct studies are small: an [EEG study presented at ACM ASSETS in 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3663547.3759743) found no significant group-level effect, with promising individual trends for unmedicated ADHD participants.

The practical reading: body doubling is cheap to test and seems to help some people much more than others. Treat any app as an experiment, not a treatment.

## How to get the most out of a session

In practice, the sessions that work have a clear structure. The app gives you a start and an end; a Pomodoro rhythm fills the middle. For a 50 or 60-minute booking:

1. **Before (2 min):** pick one small, finishable task and write it down.
2. **Check-in (1-2 min):** state your goal precisely: "draft the report intro," not "work on the report."
3. **Focus block (25 min):** that single task, phone out of reach.
4. **Break (5 min):** stand up, drink water, look away from the screen.
5. **Second block (20-25 min):** continue or start the next small task.
6. **Check-out (1-2 min):** report what you did. This is where the accountability lands.

If you book a 25-minute Focusmate session, treat it as a single Pomodoro. If you have ADHD and starting is the hard part, shorter first blocks often help; our guide to [Pomodoro and ADHD](/blog/pomodoro-adhd) and the [ADHD timer page](/pomodoro-for-adhd) explain how to adjust intervals.

On one-to-one sessions, or on days without a session, a separate solo timer keeps the rhythm. Pomodorian is one option: a free browser timer with custom intervals from 1 to 120 minutes, a task list, an AI planner, five ambient sounds, and focus stats, with no account. It runs next to your session, not inside it. ## Troubleshooting

**Your partner does not show up.** Focusmate's [help center explains](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/4044431-what-if-i-don-t-get-a-match-or-my-partner-doesn-t-show) that it tries to offer a rematch after about a minute if someone is available, and tracks no-shows automatically. Otherwise, book the next slot. In group sessions, one absence matters less.

**You feel anxious on camera.** Start where the camera is optional: Flow Club, FLOWN, StudyStream, or a Study Together room without camera rules. Dubbii's pre-recorded videos remove the audience entirely. On Focusmate, Quiet Mode removes the need to talk, though the camera stays on.

**The effect fades, or no slot fits.** Switch format (one-to-one to group), or use drop-in rooms (FLOWN Drop-In, StudyStream, Study Together) that need no booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best body doubling app for ADHD?

It depends on what gets you started. Focusmate's one-to-one format gives a strong sense of being seen, Deepwrk is built around ADHD, and Dubbii works without a live audience. None of them is a medical treatment.

### Is there a free body doubling app?

Yes. As verified on October 28, 2026: Focusmate's free plan includes 3 sessions per week, StudyStream gives about 1 hour of free focus room time per day, the Study Together Discord server is free, and FLOWN has a limited free plan.

### What is a good Focusmate alternative?

For groups instead of one-to-one, Flow Club and FLOWN. For camera-optional and free, StudyStream and Study Together. For ADHD-specific sessions, Deepwrk.

### Do I need to turn my camera on for virtual body doubling?

On Focusmate, yes. On Flow Club, FLOWN, StudyStream, and most Study Together rooms, it is optional. Dubbii's pre-recorded videos need no camera.

### Does body doubling actually work?

The evidence is mostly anecdotal and practitioner-based: CHADD says there has not been significant research to validate it, and the few direct studies are small. A free trial is a low-cost way to find out if it helps you.

## Key Takeaways

- Focusmate suits one-to-one body doubling (camera required, 3 free sessions a week); Flow Club and FLOWN suit hosted groups, and FLOWN has the longest trial here (30 days).
- Free options: Focusmate's free tier, StudyStream's daily free hour, and the Study Together Discord server.

- A Pomodoro structure (check-in, 25-minute block, break, check-out) helps turn presence into finished work.
